# Video types

Generated by `npm run docs` from `templates/types/`. Do not edit by hand.

Pick a type when you create a job. It sets the beat skeleton in `plan.json`, and `/storyboard` and `npm run check` use its pacing and retention rules.

```
npm run new -- my-video --type=saas-promo --ratio=16x9 --seconds=30
```

`--ratio` and `--seconds` are optional, and default to the type's first ratio and default length. Lengths outside the type's range are allowed, and `npm run check` warns about them. To change a type, edit its JSON file and run `npm run docs`.

These values are starting rules of thumb for this studio, not measured benchmarks.

## Case study (`case-study`)
One customer, one problem, one measured result.

- Length: 45 to 120 s, default 75 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 3.6 syllables per second
- Beat length: 4 to 12 s
- Longest static shot: 4 s
- Default transition: fade, 0.4 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| result | The headline result | 12% | `metric` | `title` |
| customer | Who they are and what they faced | 18% | `title` | `iconGrid` |
| approach | What they did | 22% | `timeline` | `stepFlow` |
| evidence | The numbers | 20% | `chart` | `metric` |
| voice | In their words | 16% | `quote` | `title` |
| cta | One action | 12% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- Lead with the result, then earn it.
- Name the customer only with written permission.
- Every number needs a source in claims.json.
- Use a real quote or none; never paraphrase into quote marks.
- Show one chart, not a dashboard.

Needs from you:
- Customer permission
- Source for every result
- Approved quote, if one is used

## Data story (`data-story`)
One insight from one dataset, built up until it lands.

- Length: 30 to 90 s, default 45 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 3.6 syllables per second
- Beat length: 4 to 12 s
- Longest static shot: 5 s
- Default transition: fade, 0.4 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| claim | The insight in one sentence | 15% | `title` | `metric` |
| context | What is being measured | 15% | `iconGrid` | `title` |
| build | The chart builds | 30% | `chart` | `timeline` |
| reveal | The mark that matters | 20% | `metric` | `chart` |
| meaning | So what, and one action | 20% | `cta` | `title` |

Retention rules:
- State the insight as a claim, not a topic.
- Build the chart in the order the voice explains it.
- One axis per chart; two measures means two charts.
- Highlight the one mark that matters.
- Show the data source on screen.

Needs from you:
- Dataset and source
- Permission to publish the numbers

## Explainer (`explainer`)
Teaches one idea to someone who does not know it yet.

- Length: 45 to 120 s, default 75 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 3.6 syllables per second
- Beat length: 4 to 12 s
- Longest static shot: 4 s
- Default transition: fade, 0.4 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hook | Ask the question or name the confusion | 10% | `title` | `quote` |
| problem | Why this matters to the viewer | 15% | `iconGrid` | `title` |
| concept | The core idea, in one picture | 25% | `stepFlow` | `deviceMockup`, `threeStage` |
| how | How it works, step by step | 25% | `timeline` | `stepFlow` |
| example | One concrete example | 15% | `beforeAfter` | `chart` |
| cta | Recap and one action | 10% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- State the question the viewer has in the first 3 seconds.
- Introduce one concept per beat and show it as it is named.
- Use an analogy or diagram before any detail.
- Change the visual at least every 4 seconds, even if only by adding an element.
- Recap the one takeaway before the CTA.

Needs from you:
- One-sentence takeaway
- Facts or script from the client

## Feature launch (`feature-launch`)
Announces one new capability to people who already know the product.

- Length: 15 to 60 s, default 30 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 4.2 syllables per second
- Beat length: 2 to 8 s
- Longest static shot: 3 s
- Default transition: slide, 0.35 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| announce | Name the new feature | 15% | `logoReveal` | `title` |
| pain | The old way, briefly | 15% | `beforeAfter` | `title` |
| show | The feature working | 40% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| benefits | Up to three outcomes | 15% | `iconGrid` | `stepFlow` |
| cta | Where to find it | 15% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- Say what is new in the first 2 seconds.
- Show the feature working, not a list of benefits.
- Name the old pain only once.
- Say where to find it.
- Use a real launch date or none at all.

Needs from you:
- Feature name and availability confirmed
- Real screens, or approval for illustrative UI

## SaaS demo (`saas-demo`)
Walks through the real product doing one job from start to finish.

- Length: 60 to 180 s, default 90 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 3.6 syllables per second
- Beat length: 4 to 14 s
- Longest static shot: 5 s
- Default transition: fade, 0.4 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| outcome | The result the viewer will get | 10% | `deviceMockup` | `title` |
| setup | Who the user is and what they need | 10% | `title` | `iconGrid` |
| step-1 | First action in the product | 20% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| step-2 | Second action, with a zoom callout | 25% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| step-3 | Third action and the result | 20% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| recap | What was done, in three steps | 8% | `stepFlow` | `iconGrid` |
| cta | One action | 7% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- Show the finished result in the opening seconds, then rewind to how.
- Follow one user and one task; do not tour the menu.
- Zoom to what matters whenever the cursor acts.
- Every cursor move needs a reason in the voiceover.
- Use real screens when supplied, and say so when UI is illustrative.

Needs from you:
- Real product screens, or written approval for illustrative UI
- One user task to follow

## SaaS promo (`saas-promo`)
Short brand-led promise for a product, built to earn a click.

- Length: 15 to 45 s, default 30 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 4.5 syllables per second
- Beat length: 1.5 to 7 s
- Longest static shot: 3 s
- Default transition: cut

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hook | Name the pain or ask the question | 15% | `title` | `quote` |
| solution | The product answers it | 30% | `deviceMockup` | `iconGrid` |
| proof | Why to believe it | 25% | `iconGrid` | `chart`, `quote` |
| turn | The change for the viewer | 15% | `beforeAfter` | `title` |
| cta | Logo and one action | 15% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- Hook with the pain or a question in the first second.
- One promise per beat, cut on the spoken word.
- Show the product within the first third.
- On-screen words are fewer than spoken words.
- End on one CTA, held long enough to read.

Needs from you:
- CTA text and URL
- Logo
- Claims confirmed by the client

## Social teaser (`social-teaser`)
Silent-first clip that stops the scroll.

- Length: 6 to 20 s, default 12 s
- Ratios: 9x16, 1x1, 16x9
- Pace limit: 5 syllables per second
- Beat length: 1 to 4 s
- Longest static shot: 2 s
- Default transition: cut

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hook | Stop the scroll on frame 0 | 25% | `title` | `metric` |
| tease | The surprising part | 40% | `iconGrid` | `beforeAfter` |
| payoff | The reason to act | 35% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- Assume no sound: captions always on.
- The first frame must already make sense.
- Change the picture at least every 2 seconds.
- Keep text clear of platform UI (see the safe zones).
- Loop or end on a reason to tap.

Needs from you:
- Platform
- Word-timed captions, if there is voiceover

## Storyline (`storyline`)
Character-led narrative: someone wants something, and something changes.

- Length: 30 to 120 s, default 60 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 3.8 syllables per second
- Beat length: 3 to 12 s
- Longest static shot: 4 s
- Default transition: fade, 0.5 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| setup | The character and their normal day | 20% | `title` | `threeStage` |
| tension | What goes wrong | 20% | `alert` | `title` |
| turn | The moment things change | 20% | `quote` | `logoReveal` |
| resolve | The new normal | 25% | `beforeAfter` | `timeline` |
| cta | Brand and one action | 15% | `cta` | `logoReveal` |

Retention rules:
- Open on the character in a moment, not on the brand.
- Show the stakes before the solution.
- Each beat changes the situation.
- Keep the brand out until the turn.
- Let the ending breathe before the CTA.

Needs from you:
- Who the character is
- What changes for them
- Approval that the story is illustrative, or real story sign-off

## Tutorial (`tutorial`)
Teaches a viewer to complete one task by themselves.

- Length: 60 to 240 s, default 120 s
- Ratios: 16x9, 9x16, 1x1
- Pace limit: 3.4 syllables per second
- Beat length: 4 to 16 s
- Longest static shot: 6 s
- Default transition: fade, 0.4 s

| Beat | Goal | Share | Scene | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| goal | What you will be able to do | 8% | `title` | `deviceMockup` |
| overview | The steps at a glance | 10% | `stepFlow` | `timeline` |
| step-1 | Step 1 | 22% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| step-2 | Step 2 | 22% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| step-3 | Step 3 | 22% | `cursorDemo` | `deviceMockup` |
| result | The finished state | 8% | `beforeAfter` | `deviceMockup` |
| next | Where to go next | 8% | `cta` | `iconGrid` |

Retention rules:
- Say what the viewer will be able to do, and what they need first.
- One step per beat, numbered on screen.
- Zoom in before anything small is clicked.
- Pause on the result of each step long enough to compare.
- End with the finished state and where to go next.

Needs from you:
- The exact steps, tested
- Real screens, or approval for illustrative UI
