# Video brief: V.E.R.O.N. promo

1. Topic and one takeaway:
   V.E.R.O.N. tracks every robot on your site, and alerts you right away if something looks off.
2. Audience:
   Security and facility operations managers running robot fleets on a single site.
3. Where it plays, length, ratio:
   Platform: web/social, no platform-specific rules
   Length (seconds): 24.92, set by the audio. Picture is 748 frames at 30 fps; the mux trims to the audio.
   Ratio: 16:9, 1920x1080
4. Reference video:
   public/jobs/veron-promo/ref.mp4 and ref-2.mp4. Pace, type and transitions only. Notes in edit/style.md.
5. Brand:
   Source is public/jobs/veron-promo/brand-guideline.pdf, filled into brand.json.
   Scene 1 may use any colours to show the night site. From scene 2 on: Ink, Slate, Cobalt, Violet, orange #FF8A1F for alerts only, Roboto, supplied backgrounds only.
   brand.json keys with no direct guideline role: bg and panel hold the guideline background colours as fallbacks, ok holds Cobalt, warn and error hold the alert orange.
6. CTA:
   Button text: Request a Demo
   URL: proctortrack.com/veron
7. Script and audio:
   script.md. Audio file: voiceover.mp3 (copy of "Hale v1.mp3"). The audio and its transcript are the source of truth.
8. Claims needing client confirmation:
   Spoken in the audio: tracks every robot on the site (who, where, what it is doing, how it is performing); alerts right away; "never monitoring alone"; every robot and route verified.
   The logo file carries the tagline "Human Intelligence for Robotics Operations", which is not in the script.
   "Certified" engineers are not claimed anywhere in this video, by decision.

UI is illustrative, not real product screens. The robots, engineer avatar, icons, dashboard, site map and facility are drawn in code and generic. Telemetry is bars only, with no invented numbers or robot IDs.

Logo decisions: uncropped file at about 140 px on beats 2 to 4 and large on beat 5. No logo on beat 1, an accepted exception to the guideline, because the navy wordmark would disappear on the night scene.
Background: Soft Lavender Wave Gradient on beats 2 to 5.
Contrast: orange only on icons and cards, captions stay Ink, Cobalt only on lighter zones.
